The finding that changes the target

Most remaining brands do not publish print settings at all. Of the 59 brands on custom platforms, a generic multilingual parser reached only 13. The other 39 were checked individually and yield nothing extractable — not because parsing failed, but because the data is not on the page.

This is a ceiling on the whole approach, not a to-do item. Reading those TDS PDFs is the one avenue that would add meaningful coverage — the fetch layer already has pdftotext support and it is unused so far.
