Inheritance — why 46% of slicer rows would otherwise be wrong

Orca profiles chain through inherits, and a leaf file is usually incomplete. 227 of 498 rows required walking that chain.

LevelProfileWhat it actually defines
leafBambu PLA Basic @baseNo temperature field at all. Defines MVS 21, density 1.26
parentfdm_filament_pla220 / 55 — and MVS 12, density 1.24, both overridden by the leaf
rootfdm_filament_commonShared machinery

A naive reader takes the leaf at face value and emits nothing, or takes the root and emits the wrong flow rate for every Bambu filament.
